# What's new in SeamDrop

Each version's notes appear in the app's update notice ("What's new: …") and on its release page, so keep them short and in plain words.

## 0.5.0-beta.2
- Settings work again: Appearance, Get test builds and the other switches now save.
- If something doesn't work, the window says so, and Details says why.
- The drop area says where files go again, and About no longer shows an error while there are only test builds.

## 0.5.0-beta.1
- A test build. Update all your computers: 0.5 can't talk to 0.3.
- Pair by typing a code, send to several computers at once, and keep text on the Shelf, shared with the computers you choose.
- Transfers pause within seconds when a computer goes away, and continue by themselves when it's back.

## 0.4.0
(Never published: its changes are in 0.5.0-beta.1.)
- Update both computers: pairing is now by typing a code, and 0.4 can't talk to 0.3.
- Use SeamDrop with all your computers: tick where files and the clipboard go, and send to several at once.
- The Shelf keeps what was sent to you; text can be sent as private, kept out of clipboard history.

## 0.3.1
- Esc closes Settings and the clipboard list.

## 0.3.0
- SeamDrop is Portal's new name. Your settings, paired devices and keys come over by themselves.
- A transfer that loses its connection pauses and picks up where it stopped, also after a restart or sleep.
- Share the clipboard: Send clipboard puts what you copied on the other computer, ready to paste.
- SeamDrop keeps itself up to date. It restarts only when you say so, and never during a transfer.
